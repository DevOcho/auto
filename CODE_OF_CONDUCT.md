# Code of conduct

auto is a tool for getting work done. Keep conversations here about the work.

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, insults, or posting other people's private
  details.
- Stay on topic; take other debates elsewhere.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who
don't follow this.

To report a problem privately, email [info@devocho.com](mailto:info@devocho.com).
